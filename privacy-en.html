Privacy policy

Updated: November 13, 2026

Cyclo Manager (com.cyclomanager.app) is a cycling management game you can play offline, published by Theguiguix. This policy describes what data is used, by whom, and how to control it.

Who is responsible

The publisher of the app is Theguiguix. The game has no user account and no career server: your game is stored on your device.

For a question about your data, write to Contact_GT.Solutions@proton.me, or use the “Leave this club” button in Settings to erase the local save.

Data we store on the device

The career (team, calendar, finances, settings, ad-free pass purchase) is saved locally in the app storage. This information is not sent to any Cyclo Manager server: we do not run one.

You can erase this save at any time: Settings → Career → Leave this club, or by uninstalling the app.

Ads (players without the pass)

Without the ad-free pass, Google AdMob may show an interstitial after some races, and a rewarded ad you choose to watch (to draw bonus goals in the off-season). These services are provided by Google, not by our servers.

AdMob may process an advertising ID, an approximate location (derived from the IP address), technical device data, diagnostic data (crashes, performance) and ad interaction data, depending on the consent you give (UMP consent message in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom).

Offline, or if you bought the pass, no ad is requested. The trial race shows no interstitial.

Audience measurement (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To understand how the game is played and improve it (difficulty, moments when players stop), the app sends a few anonymous events to Google Analytics for Firebase: career started, trial race and first race completed, season completed (number, division, rank), division promotion or relegation, bankruptcy, rewarded ad watched, answer to the notification request.

These events contain neither your name, nor your club name, nor your email. Firebase links them to an app installation ID (reset if you uninstall the game) and may derive technical data from them (device model, Android version, approximate country). The advertising ID is not used by Firebase.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, this measurement is only enabled if you accept it in the consent message shown at launch (purpose “store and/or access information on a device”). Without your agreement, no event is recorded. The data is processed by Google as a processor and kept according to the default Google Analytics settings.

Notifications

If you allow it, the game schedules local notifications on your phone: a reminder after a few days without playing. They are created by the device itself, with no server and no data sent. You can turn them off in the Android settings.

Review requests

From time to time, the game may open the official Google Play window to suggest rating the app. Your rating and review are handled by Google Play; the app does not receive them.

In-app purchases

The lifetime ad-free pass is a one-time purchase handled by Google Play Billing. Google is responsible for payment, invoicing and purchase restoration. The app only keeps a local “pass active” flag, with no card number or Google account ID.

Data we do not collect

No Cyclo Manager account, no mandatory email address, no geolocation, no contacts, no photos, no ad tracking by us, no crash log sent by us. The audience measurement described above is the only usage data collected.

Legal basis (GDPR)

Game save: legitimate interest / performance of the requested service (running the game offline).

Personalised ads: consent where the law requires it. Non-personalised ads or no ads: according to your choice (refusal, offline, pass).

Audience measurement: consent in the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland; elsewhere, legitimate interest in improving the game.

Pass purchase: performance of the sales contract through Google Play.

Your rights

You can access your career data by playing, erase it (new career or uninstall), and manage the advertising ID in the Android settings. In the EEA / UK, the consent form (ads and audience measurement) may be shown again at launch if the status requires it.

For a GDPR request (access, erasure, objection), write to Contact_GT.Solutions@proton.me. Advertising data held by Google can also be managed in your Google account and the Android settings.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 16 and over. It is not designed for children and no data is knowingly collected from them. Ads follow the Google Play and AdMob policies.

Changes

This policy may be updated. The effective date is shown at the top of the document (currently November 13, 2026). An up-to-date version is always available in Settings.
