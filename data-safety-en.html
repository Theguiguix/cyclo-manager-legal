Data safety sheet

Updated: November 13, 2026

This sheet restates, in plain language, the declarations made in the Google Play Data safety form. It describes what the app and its partner SDKs do — not data gathered on our servers (there are none).

Overview

Collection: no data sent to a Cyclo Manager server. Data is collected by the built-in Google SDKs: AdMob (ads) and Google Analytics for Firebase (anonymous audience measurement, with consent in the EEA / UK / Switzerland).

Sharing: yes, only through Google AdMob (ads) for players without the pass, when the network is available and consent allows it. Firebase acts as a processor: its data is not shared with third parties.

Data sold by the publisher: no. Encryption in transit: yes, all collected data goes over HTTPS (the app makes no network calls of its own). Deletion: yes (local save + uninstall + request by email).

Target audience: 16 and over. The app is not aimed at children.

Data collected — Device identifiers

Android advertising ID (AD_ID permission): used by Google AdMob to serve and measure ads. Not stored by Cyclo Manager. Can be reset in the phone settings.

Purpose: advertising. Optional: yes (ad-free pass, offline, or consent refused in the UMP message).

Firebase installation ID (app instance ID): specific to the app, unrelated to the advertising ID, reset on uninstall. Purpose: analytics. Collected, not shared.

Data collected — Financial information

In-app purchase history: handled by Google Play (ad-free pass). Cyclo Manager only receives the purchase / restore status and stores “pass active” locally.

Purpose: purchase processing. Bank details: never read by the app.

Data collected — App activity

Ad interactions (display, close): handled by AdMob to serve ads. Purpose: advertising. Absent with the pass / offline / trial race (no interstitial).

App interactions: 9 anonymous game events sent to Google Analytics for Firebase (career started, trial race, first official race, season completed, promotion or relegation, bankruptcy, rewarded ad watched, answer to notifications, end-of-mandate choice). No name, club name or email. Purpose: analytics. Collected, not shared, optional in the EEA / UK / Switzerland (refusal in the consent message).

Notifications: local, scheduled on the device, no data sent.

Data not collected

Precise location, contacts, photos, files, SMS, calendar, health, audio, call log, real name, email, app list, diagnostics / crashes sent by the publisher (the Google Play and AdMob SDKs may process some on their side, see above).

Security

The game stays on the device. AdMob, Firebase and Play Billing traffic goes over HTTPS. The publisher does not upload the save to the cloud.

“Independent security review” commitment: no (indie game, no declared external audit).

How to delete data

Career save: Settings → Career → Leave this club, or uninstall.

Audience measurement: uninstalling the app resets the Firebase ID; an erasure request can be sent to Contact_GT.Solutions@proton.me.

Advertising ID: Android settings → Privacy / Google → Reset advertising ID.

Purchases: managed by Google Play (account history). The pass can be restored on another device with “Restore purchase”.
